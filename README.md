### On AI in delivery

I think the interesting question isn't *whether* a leader uses AI — it's *where they draw the line* between what gets delegated to it and what stays a human decision with human hands on it. That's a governance call, not a technical one, and I try to make it explicit in my own work rather than leave it implied.
